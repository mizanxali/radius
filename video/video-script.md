# Radius launch video: script

**Length:** about 76 s. **Format:** 16:9, 1920×1080, 60 fps. The centred layout also crops to 1:1 and 9:16 without re-editing.
**Sound:** a music bed with UI sound effects, each landing on the frame of the visual event that causes it (see the sound palette below). The captions tell the whole story, so it works muted.
**Look:** mock UI rebuilt from scratch, not screen-captured. A black phone frame sits in the centre of a dark, slow-moving gradient wallpaper. Type is Cabinet Grotesk, white on black, to match the app. Colour appears only on avatars, icons and dots, as in the app.
**Touch:** a soft white touch dot moves on eased paths and lands each tap exactly on the beat (this is the phone version of the choreographed cursor).
**Camera:** the frame never stops moving. A slow Ken Burns drift runs between beats, and punch-ins follow the action Screen Studio-style. Waits are speed-ramped and key moments play at 1×.
**Cast:** demo-mode attendees. You are **Sam Okafor, Founder · Driftwood**. The room includes Priya Raman (General Partner, Lattice Ventures, 🐋), Leo Martins (Protocol Engineer, Zephyr Labs) and Hana Sato (Product Designer).

### Caption band

- **Position:** the bottom 20% of the frame, which stays fixed and is unaffected by the camera. In the 1:1 and 9:16 crops it sits at the same distance from the bottom.
- **Scrim:** a soft black gradient from 0% to about 70% behind the band, so white text stays readable over light UI.
- **Two lines, centred:** the title in `F.bold` (about 56 px) and the description in regular weight (about 36 px, 80% white). Keep the description to about 8 words, since people read it while watching.
- **Framing rule:** the band covers the bottom of the phone during punch-ins, so aim each punch-in so the action sits above the band. When the target is low on the phone (the tab bar, the share sheet, the **Wave** and **We met** buttons), move the camera down so the phone rides up.

### Sound palette

Keep the same sound for the same event every time, so the viewer learns them.

| Sound | Plays on |
|-------|----------|
| **Tap** (soft click) | Every touch-dot tap. |
| **Key clicks** | Each character typed (website, note). Light and slightly varied in pitch, never a loop. |
| **Whoosh** | Something leaving your phone: a wave sent, the meet countersign, the export. |
| **Join chime** | Someone appearing: avatars landing on the radar, Priya's phone joining the split-screen. |
| **Thud** (low haptic hit) | A phone buzzing: a wave arriving, each Finder pulse. |
| **Ticker** | Numbers counting up (net worth, the room). It stops dead on the final number. |
| **Stamp** | The mint landing. It is the biggest sound in the video, so use it only here. |
| **Swish** (quiet) | Each title landing and shrinking into the band. Keep it under the music. |

**Mix:** duck the music by about 3 dB under the two hero moments (wave back, stamp minted) so the sound effects carry them. If several sounds would stack, keep the one tied to the main action and drop the rest.

### The repeating feature beat (about 6 s each)

1. **0.0 s:** the title lands in big kinetic type, centred over the phone. The phone dims to about 40%.
2. **0.6 s:** the title shrinks and glides down into the caption band, and the description types in beneath it. At the same time the phone undims and the camera punches in on the action (scale about 1.8×, ease-in-out, 0.5 s).
3. **1.0–5.4 s:** the feature plays out with the caption on screen the whole time.
4. **5.4 s:** the camera punches out to the centred phone, and the caption fades down and out. The next title lands straight away.

Hard-cut the phone's screen content between beats so you never animate between tabs, which keeps it snappy.

## 0:00–0:05 · Cold open

**Screen:** pure black. Kinetic type, centred, large, one word at a time on the beat:

> **the** · **ultimate** · **networking** · **tool**

Each word snaps up from a 12 px offset with a slight blur-to-sharp. Hold for 0.6 s, then the whole line shrinks and fades up and out.

**SFX:** a low, muted hit on each word, timed to the music beat. Bring the music in fully on "tool".

## 0:05–0:08 · Phone enters

The phone slides up from below the frame into the centre with a spring and a little overshoot. The app is open on **Radar** in demo mode: avatars drift on the rings and a sweep turns. The camera eases in a touch as the phone settles.

**Caption band:** `Radius`, then it fades as the first title lands.

**SFX:** a soft air rise as the phone slides up, then a run of quiet join chimes as the avatars pop onto the radar one by one.

---

## 0:08–0:14 · 1. Radar

> **Everyone nearby**
> Placed by Bluetooth signal. No accounts, no Wi-Fi.

**Punch-in:** the radar fills the frame. Avatars move closer and further as the signal changes. The touch dot taps the **Good fit** filter chip, which dims the rest of the room and leaves three avatars lit. Tap **Priya Raman**, and her pass slides up. Pan up the pass past **Building**, **Looking for** and **Can offer** so that each section passes above the band.

**SFX:** a tap on **Good fit**, then a soft "dim" whoosh as the rest of the room fades. A tap on Priya, then a light swipe as her pass slides up.

## 0:14–0:21 · 2. AI match

> **AI reads the room**
> Claude picks who to meet, and why.

**Punch-in:** the **AI match** tab and its **Talk to these first** list. Cards cascade in with their one-line reasons. Ramp the speed through the scroll, then return to 1× and tap **Write an icebreaker** on Leo. Show **Writing an icebreaker…**, then cut to the result under **Say this**, typed out word by word. Push in further on the text.

**SFX:** a soft tick as each card lands. A tap on **Write an icebreaker**, then a faint shimmer under **Writing an icebreaker…**, and quiet key clicks as the icebreaker types out.

## 0:21–0:27 · 3. AI summary

> **No more pitch decks**
> Claude reads your site and says what you do.

**Punch-in:** the **Edit pass** screen. The touch dot taps **Website** and types `driftwood.xyz`, and the camera pushes in as it types (frame the field high, because the keyboard comes up into the band). Save, then hard-cut to the pass, where the **AI summary** block fades in with its AI label visible.

**SFX:** a tap on **Website**, key clicks for every character of `driftwood.xyz`, a tap on **Save**, then a soft shimmer as the summary appears.

## 0:27–0:34 · 4. Verified net worth

> **Flex your bags, provably**
> Wallet-signed net worth. Show the number or your tier.

**Punch-in:** tap **Connect wallet** and show **Check your wallet…**. Ramp through the signature. **Ethereum · verified** ticks in and the net worth counts up quickly from $0. Flip the **Show exact amount** toggle off, and the number morphs into the tier badge.

**Hero cutaway (1 s):** the camera leaves the phone for a row of tiers above the band, 🦐 🦀 🐟 🐬 🦈 🐋, with the 🐋 popping. The caption stays.

**SFX:** a tap on **Connect wallet**, then a crisp confirm click when **Ethereum · verified** ticks in. The ticker runs under the count-up, a tap and a toggle click on **Show exact amount**, a pop on each tier emoji, and a slightly bigger pop on the 🐋.

## 0:34–0:40 · 5. The room

> **Know the room**
> Total net worth, a leaderboard, and who wants what.

**Punch-in:** the **The room** tab. **Net worth in the room** counts up. The category bars grow in with their counts, and the leaderboard rows stagger in. Pan slowly down to **Most wanted**, **The room wants** and **The room offers**.

**SFX:** the ticker under **Net worth in the room**, then soft rising plucks as the category bars grow and a light tick per leaderboard row.

## 0:40–0:47 · 6. Wave (the split-screen "aha")

> **Say hi without saying hi**
> One tap buzzes their phone.

**Split:** instead of punching in, the camera pulls back slightly. Sam's phone slides left and Priya's phone slides in from the right, so the two sit side by side above the band. On the left phone, tap **Wave**. Priya's phone shakes (haptic thud) and the banner drops: **Sam waved at you**. Her touch dot taps **Wave back**, and the banner lands on the left phone. Play it at 1× with no ramp, because this beat sells the product.

**SFX:** a join chime as Priya's phone slides in. A tap on **Wave** with a whoosh panned left to right, and a thud as her phone shakes. A tap on **Wave back**, with the whoosh panned right to left and a softer thud on Sam's phone. Duck the music here.

Both phones stay on screen for beats 7 and 8.

## 0:47–0:53 · 7. Finder

> **Hot or cold**
> Follow their signal through the crowd.

**Punch-in on the left phone:** the Finder screen. Text and colour move through **Cold, keep moving** → **Warmer** → **Getting warmer** → **Right here**. Each step brings a pulse and a slight camera shake. Punch out between the steps so you can see the two phones sliding toward each other. The physical gap closing is the joke.

**SFX:** a thud on every pulse, getting faster and higher in pitch as it warms up. Land a bright ping on **Right here**.

## 0:53–1:01 · 8. Proof of meet

> **Proof you met**
> Both phones sign. Minted onchain on Base.

**Split-screen:** the two phones are now touching in the centre. Tap **We met** on the left. The meet prompt appears on the right and Priya's touch dot confirms. A line of light arcs between the phones (the countersign). Both show **You met · minting your stamp…**, then **You met · stamp minted on Base** at the same moment.

**Hero cutaway (1 s):** the stamp badge, big and centred above the band, with the EAS attestation UID ticking in underneath.

Priya's phone then slides back out to the right, and Sam's phone recentres.

**SFX:** a tap on **We met** and a tap on Priya's confirm. A whoosh follows the arc of light between the phones, and a soft rising tone runs under **minting your stamp…**. Hit the stamp exactly on **stamp minted on Base**, with the music ducked. Use a quieter whoosh as Priya's phone leaves.

## 1:01–1:07 · 9. Notes and export

> **Remember everyone**
> Private notes. Export everyone you met.

**Punch-in:** on Priya's pass, tap **Private note** and type `pre-seed, follow up after demo day`. Hard-cut to **Me** and **People you've met**. Tap **Export**, and the share sheet slides up with the plain-text list peeking out. Move the camera down for the share sheet so the list sits above the band.

**SFX:** a tap on **Private note**, key clicks for the note, a tap on **Export**, then a whoosh as the share sheet rises.

---

## 1:07–1:10 · Exit

Punch out to the full frame and fade the caption band. The phone slides down off the frame, the reverse of the entrance (ease-in, 0.6 s). Only the wallpaper remains.

**SFX:** a soft air fall as the phone drops. The music thins out to its last phrase.

## 1:10–1:16 · Close

Kinetic type, centred, word by word as in the opening:

> **built on** · **Offline Protocol**

Hold. Underneath, smaller, fade in: `Bluetooth mesh · no accounts · no servers for discovery`

**SFX:** the same muted hits as the opening, one per word, then a final join chime as the tagline fades in.

Cut to black on the last beat of the music.
